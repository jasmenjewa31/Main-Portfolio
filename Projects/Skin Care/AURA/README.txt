AURA - The Forest Collection

GETTING STARTED
Extract the entire ZIP, then open index.html in a modern browser.
Video and images are embedded in index.html; it also works on its own.

FILES
index.html - complete website with inline styles and interactions
assets/aura-forest-film.mp4 - original header video
assets/aura-forest-stream.png - forest background image
assets/aura-natural-collection.png - product photography
README.txt - this guide

The assets folder contains separate copies of the original video and images.
No installation or build tools are required. Google Fonts load from the internet;
fallback fonts are used offline. Background motion can be paused, and the site
respects reduced-motion preferences.

This is a design prototype. The shopping bag does not process payments.
Email signup does not store or send addresses. Bag and theme choices are not
saved after reloading.

TO PUBLISH
Upload index.html and the assets folder together to a static website host.
