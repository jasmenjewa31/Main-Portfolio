HOW TO OPEN YOUR PORTFOLIO

1. Right-click the ZIP file and select Extract All.
2. Open the extracted Jayshree-Jewa-Portfolio folder.
3. Double-click index.html to open your website in your browser.

Do not open index.html directly inside the ZIP.
Keep index.html, styles.css, gallery.js, motion.js and the assets folder together.
Internet access is needed for the Google Fonts; a serif fallback is available offline.

To publish: upload all the website files and the assets folder together to your web host.
Social links and booking destinations still need your contact details before publication.
